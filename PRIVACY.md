# Privacy / الخصوصية

Islamic TV stores your confirmed city, coordinates, time zone, calculation method, minute corrections and preferences locally on the TV. Automatic Android app-data backup is disabled. No developer account, analytics, advertisements or tracking SDK is included.

Manual search sends the typed city query to Open-Meteo Geocoding. Approximate automatic detection is an explicit button: IPWhois sees the connection IP and returns a suggested city and zone. You must confirm the suggestion before it changes anything; VPN/mobile/provider routing may suggest the wrong city. No GPS, microphone or Android location permission is requested. The app does not store or log the IP itself.

Weather/dust requests send the selected coordinates and time zone to Open-Meteo/CAMS. Calculated prayer requests send selected coordinates, date, method and zone to AlAdhan. Karbala official mode fetches the Abbas shrine public page. Providers see the IP as part of normal HTTPS requests and operate under their own policies; the developer does not proxy these requests or receive the profile.

Data requests are bounded and cached. Weather/dust fetch while visible at most every30minutes; a system-managed daily job may refresh prayer times for enabled audio. Preview data remains synthetic and does not overwrite actual caches. Local content and images work offline; unavailable or stale timing/weather is labelled rather than invented.

تُحفظ المدينة والمنطقة الزمنية والطريقة والتصحيحات على التلفزيون. التحديد عبر الإنترنت تقريبي ويحتاج تأكيدك، وقد يخطئ مع VPN. البحث والطقس والمواقيت يرسلون البيانات اللازمة إلى مزوّدي الخدمات المذكورين، ولا يوجد خادم تتبّع تابع للمشروع. لا نطلب GPS أو ميكروفوناً. يمكنك تغيير المدينة أو مسح بيانات التطبيق من إعدادات التلفزيون.
